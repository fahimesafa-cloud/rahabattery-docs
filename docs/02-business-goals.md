# Business Goals

The primary business goals of the Rahabattery website are defined as follows:

## 1. Lead Generation
- Encourage users to contact the business via phone or messaging platforms
- Collect customer inquiries through clear call-to-action elements

## 2. Brand Presence
- Establish a professional and trustworthy online presence
- Clearly communicate the company’s products and services

## 3. Customer Accessibility
- Provide easy access to contact information
- Ensure the website is usable on both desktop and mobile devices

## 4. Growth Support
- Support future marketing and SEO activities
- Allow easy content updates as the business expands
